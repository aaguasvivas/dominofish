### Deal layout

A deal is the 28 tiles in Capi's layout (docs/RULES.md R3): seats in order (2v2 n, e, s, w; 1v1 n, s), seven tiles each in hand order, then the 1v1 boneyard, whose last tile is drawn first. Tiles are written low pip first, as "a-b" in text and [a, b] in JSON.

### mulberry32

Copied from packages/engine/__tests__/invariants.fuzz.test.ts:17-26 at the pin, with the type annotations removed, into tools/capi-oracle/lib.mjs. Oracle scripts pass it as the rng of createInitialState and startNewRound, as Capi's fuzz does at invariants.fuzz.test.ts:357-360, so a seed reproduces Capi's deals. tools/capi-oracle/selftest.mjs checks the copy against the four whole-suit seeds of invariants.fuzz.test.ts:460 (59313, 83322, 282144, 645301), whose 2v2 deals give the 6-6 holder all seven sixes.

```js
export function mulberry32(seed) {
  let a = seed >>> 0;
  return () => {
    a |= 0;
    a = (a + 0x6d2b79f5) | 0;
    let t = Math.imul(a ^ (a >>> 15), 1 | a);
    t = (t + Math.imul(t ^ (t >>> 7), 61 | t)) ^ t;
    return ((t ^ (t >>> 14)) >>> 0) / 4294967296;
  };
}
```

### Fixed deals

To make Capi deal a wanted order, lib.mjs's fixedDealRng(order) returns an rng for Capi's Fisher-Yates shuffle (reducer.ts:51-58): for i from 27 down to 1 it returns (j + 0.5) / (i + 1), where j is the current index of the tile wanted at position i, and it applies the same swap to its own copy of the array. dealFixedRound calls startNewRound on a round_over state whose lastCallout is "domino" and lastPlayedBy is the wanted leader (as invariants.fuzz.test.ts:488-499 does), so the round starts on an empty board with that leader and the wanted hands and boneyard. selftest.mjs checks it on 1,000 seeded orders (500 per mode).

## Golden traces

fixtures/golden/*.txt are generated by tools/capi-oracle/traces.mjs from Capi's engine at the pin and never edited by hand; crates/dominofish-core/tests/golden.rs replays them without Capi. Each file holds games in one mode and target; fixtures/golden/coverage.txt holds the rule-path counts (docs/RULES.md, Rule paths) per mode.

```bash
/Users/Adelson/.nvm/versions/node/v20.20.0/bin/node tools/capi-oracle/traces.mjs
```

Sets: 150 games per mode at target 100 and at 200 with the "raw" policy (uniform over every intent Capi accepts, so leads go left and right in both orientations), seeds 100000 + i (1v1, 100), 200000 + i (1v1, 200), 300000 + i (2v2, 100), 400000 + i (2v2, 200); and the four whole-suit seeds in 2v2 at 100 and at 200 with the policy of Capi's fuzz. Every game deals with mulberry32(seed) and chooses with mulberry32(seed ^ 0x9e3779b9), as Capi's fuzz does. The deal order is rebuilt from the 27 values Capi's shuffle drew and checked against Capi's dealt state.

Lines (lines starting with # are comments):
- `G <mode> <target> <seed> <policy>`: a game starts.
- `D <i0>,...,<i27>`: a round's deal as tile indices (docs/RULES.md R1) in the deal layout above.
- `O <seat> <a>-<b>`: round 1's opener and automatic tile.
- `S <state>`: the canonical state after the deal (and the automatic tile).
- `M <seat> <intent> | <event> | <state>`: one move. The intent is `play <a>-<b> <left|right>` (the tile as the intent orients it), `draw`, or `pass`. The event is `-`, `domino <seat> <points>`, `capicua <seat> <points with the bonus>`, `tranque <blocker> <rival> <blocker pips> <rival pips> <winner> <points>`, `corrido <team>`, or `salida <team>`; teams are 0 (n and s in 2v2, n in 1v1) and 1.
- `E`: the game ended.

The canonical state (docs/RULES.md, Canonical state) is 15 space-separated fields: phase (P playing, R round_over, F finished), score of team 0, score of team 1, the seat on turn, starterThisRound, lastPlayedBy (- for none), the pass counter, the hand masks of n, e, s, w in hex (bit i is tile index i; 0 for the unused seats in 1v1), the board mask in hex, the left and right ends (- - on an empty board), and the boneyard length.

## Edge scenarios

fixtures/edges.txt is generated by tools/capi-oracle/edges.mjs from Capi's engine at the pin and never edited by hand; crates/dominofish-core/tests/edges.rs replays it. It uses the golden-trace lines plus:
- `C <name> <cite>`: a scenario starts; the name is the one docs/RULES.md (Edge cases) maps to a test.
- `L <leader> <score0> <score1>`: the round is a later round, dealt by startNewRound with this leader and these start scores; without L the round is round 1 from createInitialState.
- `X <seat> <intent> | rejected`: Capi rejects this intent at the current state; Rust must reject it too.
- `Z`: the scenario ended (its round is over).

The G line's policy is `raw` for a round found by searching seeded deals (the deal is a seeded shuffle; choices use mulberry32(seed ^ 0x9e3779b9)) or `script` for a fixed or replayed round.

## Perft

fixtures/perft.txt is generated by tools/capi-oracle/perft.mjs from Capi's engine at the pin and never edited by hand; crates/dominofish-core/tests/perft.rs matches it (docs/RULES.md, Perft). Each query is one line:

`Q <id> <mode> <target> <start> deal=<i0>,...,<i27> path=<moves or -> depth=<d or all> total=<lines> divide=<move>:<lines>,...`

The start is `round1` (createInitialState) or `later:<leader>:<score0>:<score1>` (startNewRound). The path is the canonical moves played from the start to reach the counted position. Moves are written `<a>-<b>L` or `<a>-<b>R` (a tile, low pip first, and its canonical end), `D` (draw), or `P` (pass). Sets: per mode, 10 seeded deals (seeds 500000 + k in 2v2 and 600000 + k in 1v1; five round-1 deals and five later rounds) at depths 1 to 9, and one late position per deal (2v2 with at most 16 tiles in hands, 1v1 with at most 12 and a boneyard of at most 4) counted to the end of the round.

## Differential fuzzer

`dominofish capi-fuzz --mode <1v1|2v2> --rounds N --seed S`, run from the repository root, spawns `node tools/capi-oracle/fuzz.mjs <mode> N S`, which plays N independent rounds through Capi's engine and writes one line per round start and per move; the Rust side replays each move through the referee. Round r draws everything from mulberry32((S ^ imul(r + 1, 0x9e3779b9)) >>> 0): the target (100 or 200), the policy (raw twice as often as heavy, light, or blocker), round 1 (a quarter of rounds, createInitialState) or a later round (startNewRound) with start scores biased to the bonus cap and the target edges, the deal, and in 2v2 about 1 round in 500 where the leader holds a whole suit and leads its double. So a run with fewer rounds is a prefix of a longer run with the same seed.

Lines:
- `R <r> <r1|L> <starter> <score0> <score1> <target> <i0>,...,<i27>`: a round starts (seat indices: 2v2 n 0, e 1, s 2, w 3; 1v1 n 0, s 1).
- `<move> <lo> <hi> <rawlo> <rawhi> <digest>`: one move. The move is `<a><b><l|r>` (the tile as Capi's intent orients it and the end), `d`, or `p`. lo and hi (hex) are Capi's canonical legal-move set before the move: bit 2 * tile + end (0 left, 1 right) for plays, 56 for draw, 57 for pass, split into bits 0 to 31 and 32 to 57. rawlo and rawhi are the raw set in the same encoding: every intent Capi accepts (each tile in both orientations on both ends, draw, pass) mapped to a move by the exact-replay map (docs/RULES.md, Capi intents to moves). Rust compares it with every move its is_legal accepts, a double on an empty board listed once (left), so the referee accepts exactly what Capi accepts. The digest (hex) is a word-wise FNV-1a with a final avalanche over the canonical state after the move (phase, scores, turn, opener, last player with 4 for none, passes, four hand masks, board mask, left and right ends with 7 for an empty board, boneyard length) followed by the event words (0 none; 1 domino seat points; 2 capicua seat points; 3 tranque blocker rival blocker-pips rival-pips winner points; 4 corrido team; 5 salida team).
- `P <path> <count>`: rule-path counts (docs/RULES.md, Rule paths) from Capi's transitions.
- `END <rounds> <moves>`.

A mismatch prints an ERROR line with the seed and round; the rest of that round is skipped.

## Game records

A game record is a text file of one game: each round's deal and its moves in order (core/src/record.rs, GameRecord::to_text and from_text). Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored. Example:

```
dominofish-record 1
mode 2v2
target 100
deal 0-6 0-4 0-0 5-6 1-5 2-6 0-1 5-5 6-6 3-6 4-6 2-5 2-4 3-3 1-4 1-1 4-5 0-3 0-5 2-3 4-4 1-3 1-2 3-4 0-2 2-2 1-6 3-5
s pass
w play 1-6 left
n play 1-5 left
...
deal ...
...
end
```

- `dominofish-record 1`, `mode <1v1|2v2>`, `target <1 to 300>`: the header, in this order.
- `deal <28 tiles>`: a round's deal in Capi's tile order and layout (docs/RULES.md R1 and R3): seven tiles per seat in seat order n, e, s, w (1v1: n, s), then the 1v1 boneyard, whose last tile is drawn first. Tiles are written `a-b` (the parser accepts either pip first; the printer writes the low pip first). Round 1's automatic tile is not a move: the referee places it from the deal.
- `<seat> play <a-b> <left|right>`, `<seat> draw`, `<seat> pass`: one move of the seat on turn. On an empty board `left` places the tile low pip left and `right` places it flipped (docs/RULES.md, Moves). A draw's tiles follow from the deal.
- `end`: the record ends.

The parser replays the record and rejects, with the line number, a move by a seat not on turn, an illegal move, a deal before the previous round is over, and a deal after the game ended. The last round may be unfinished (a position under analysis).

## Engine protocol

Engines run as separate processes; the referee writes one line per message to an engine's stdin and reads one line per reply from its stdout (core/src/protocol.rs). Seats are named n, e, s, w (2v2) or n, s (1v1); tiles `a-b`; lists of tiles are comma-separated. An engine receives only its own hand, its own drawn tiles, and public moves.

Referee to engine, in game order:
- `dominofish 1`: the protocol version. Reply `dominofish 1 ready budgets=<b> threads=<n> tables=<t> view=<infoset|omniscient>` with the engine's own settings (- for none) and what it sees; the engines of one side must report the same settings. Every fair engine says view=infoset; only the omniscient bot, a cheating upper bound (M3-04), says view=omniscient.
- `header <text>`: the match header (each side's command, commit, Bench, binary hash, and reported settings; mode, unit, target, pairs; M2-06). It holds no seed. Reply with the same line, `header <text>`, before the first deal.
- `game mode=<1v1|2v2> target=<t> seat=<seat> seed=<u64>`: a new game; the seed is the engine's own randomness and is no function of the deal.
- `round <r> scores=<s0>,<s1> leader=<seat> hand=<tiles>`: round r starts (from 1); the engine's dealt hand in deal order; the leader is round 1's opener or the previous round's winner.
- `opening <seat> <a-b>`: round 1's automatic tile.
- `drew <tiles>`: to the drawer only, the tiles it drew in draw order, before the public draw line.
- `played <seat> play <a-b> <left|right>`, `played <seat> draw <count>`, `played <seat> pass`: every move, to every engine, the mover's own included.
- `infoset <text>`: before each `go`, the InfoSet the referee builds for the engine's seat (InfoSet text, below). An engine decides from it alone.
- `state <state text>`: before each `go`, after the infoset, and only to an engine that said view=omniscient: the full state, every hidden tile and the 1v1 boneyard order included (State text, below). No other engine ever receives it.
- `go`: the engine's turn. Reply `move play <a-b> <left|right>`, `move draw`, or `move pass`.
- `roundover scores=<s0>,<s1>` and `gameover scores=<s0>,<s1>`: a round or the game ended.
- `quit`: exit.

An engine may also write `info <text>` lines at any time; the referee ignores them, except that it counts `info capped` (a search that reached its node cap) for the result file, and it stops the match with a Sampler fault at `info inconsistent` (a PIMC engine drew a world that disagrees with its InfoSet) or `info fallback` (it could draw no world; docs/PLAN.md D17). Any other line must be a reply to the message before it: before each `go` the referee reads the lines already waiting, and a line other than `info` there is a protocol fault. Each reply has one deadline (--timeout-ms), and `info` lines do not extend it.

## InfoSet text

InfoSet::to_text writes what one seat knows as one line of space-separated key=value fields (core/src/infoset.rs); equal InfoSets give the same bytes, and InfoSet::from_text reads it back. Never-peeks (M2-05) compares these bytes; the match harness (M2-06) sends the line to an engine as `infoset <text>` before each `go`.

`v=1 mode=<1v1|2v2> target=<t> scores=<s0>,<s1> seat=<seat> hand=<tiles> board=<hex mask> ends=<lr|--> turn=<seat> starter=<seat> last=<seat|-> passes=<n> phase=<P|R|F> sizes=<n>,<e>,<s>,<w> pile=<boneyard count> opening=<seat:a-b|-> voids=<hex>,<hex>,<hex>,<hex> excluded=<hex>,<hex>,<hex>,<hex> history=<entries|->`

- hand: the seat's tiles, comma-separated, index order. ends: the two end pips as two digits, `--` on an empty board.
- voids: per seat n, e, s, w, the pips it surely lacks in its current hand, bit p for pip p. excluded: per seat, the tiles its round-1 dealt hand cannot have held (1v1), bit i for tile index i.
- history: the round's moves so far, `;`-separated, each `<seat>:<action>:<ends before>` with action `p<a-b><L|R>` (a play), `d<count>` (a draw), or `x` (a pass).

## Match results

`dominofish match` writes one file per match (the --out path: runs/ for scratch matches, docs/eval/results/ for committed runs) and prints one summary line. A match that runs longer than a minute also prints a progress line to stderr, at most once a minute: `match: <n> of at most <pairs> pairs in <s> s`, followed by `, LLR <llr> (bounds <lower> and <upper>)` when an SPRT runs. The file does not change. Build an engine binary first with `scripts/build-bin.sh <commit>`, which prints runs/bin/<commit>/dominofish; the harness refuses a binary under target/.

```bash
scripts/build-bin.sh HEAD
```

```bash
runs/bin/<commit>/dominofish match --mode 2v2 --unit round --target 100 --pairs 20 --seed 4000 --engine-seed 5000000000 --a "runs/bin/<commit>/dominofish engine --bot accompanied" --b "runs/bin/<commit>/dominofish engine --bot rule" --out runs/m.txt
```

- `# header <text>`: the match header every engine echoed: for each side its command (spaces as +), commit (the runs/bin directory, - otherwise), Bench, binary hash (FNV-1a 64 of the file), the budgets, threads, and tables its engines reported, and its view (infoset, or omniscient for the cheating upper bound); then mode, unit, target, and pairs. Bench is the last line of `<binary> bench` for a side whose engines search (those that report budgets: the omniscient bot and PIMC), - otherwise.
- `# seeds deals=<first>..<last> engines=<base>`: the deal seeds (--seed + i for pair i) and the engine seed base (--engine-seed). The referee keeps both from the engines.
- `pair <i> seed <deal seed> game1 <A>-<B> game2 <A>-<B> rounds <r1>,<r2> margin <m> wins <w>`: one duplicate pair; r1 and r2 are the rounds each game lasted. Pair i plays deal seed (--seed) + i twice, side A on team 0's seats first, then on team 1's. Round r of either game deals with derive(deal seed, r); in both games of pair i, the engine at seat s gets the seed derive(derive(engine seed base, i), 100 + s), keyed by pair and seat and apart from the deals. Files written before this rule (R1) have no seeds line and hold the deal seeds and one budgets, threads, and tables for both sides in the header; their seat seeds were derive(deal seed, 100 + seat). The scores are each side's team total at the end of the game (unit game) or of round 1 (unit round), bonuses included; margin is A minus B summed over both games; wins counts the games A finished at the target.
- `# summary <text>`: side A's points per round (each pair's margin divided by its rounds) with mean, SD, and 95% CI over pairs; for unit game also A's game-win rate (each pair's wins / 2) with its 95% CI; with `--sprt <mu0>,<mu1>`, the SPRT on the pair margin (H0 mean mu0 against H1 mean mu1, alpha = beta = 0.05; docs/PLAN.md D14): H0, H1, or inconclusive, after how many pairs, and the LLR with its bounds; with `--sprt-on wins` (unit game) the same on each pair's share of its two games won by A (0, 0.5, or 1), written `sprt on the game-win share, H0 <mu0> H1 <mu1>, ...`. With --sprt the match stops at a decision, and --pairs is the maximum; a run that reaches it is inconclusive. The summary text is also printed.
- `# fault <kind>: <detail>`: the match stopped at an illegal move, crash, timeout (--timeout-ms, default 10,000, one deadline per reply, a safety limit, not a budget), protocol error (an unreadable line, a wrong reply, a line out of turn, or settings that differ within a side), header echo that differs, or sampler fault (`info inconsistent` or `info fallback`); the match is void and the command exits 1.
- `# capped a=<n> b=<n>`: each side's searches that reached their node cap, counted from the engines' `info capped` lines (only an engine that searches sends them).
- `aivat <i> deal <d> draw <w> action <a>`: with `--aivat J`, one line per pair after the pair lines: side A's AIVAT terms summed over both games, in points (docs/PLAN.md D19): the deal terms of the rounds after the first, the 1v1 draw terms (0 when an engine sees hidden state), and the action terms of the baseline bots on the sides that the `# aivat` line's actions field names.
- `# aivat j=<J> nodes=<cap> values=<n> capped=<n> actions=<sides> draws=<on|off> <corrected summary>`: with `--aivat J`, the settings, the number of solver values computed and of those that reached the cap, which sides' moves had action terms (a, b, a,b, or -: a side gets them only when it runs a baseline bot from the referee's own binary, since the terms weigh its moves with the referee's `Bot::policy`; files written before 2026-10-30 have no actions field and gave them to every baseline bot), whether draw terms were on, and the corrected points per round: each pair's margin per round minus its term totals (the `aivat` line, not divided by the pair's rounds; D19 and Q12) times coefficients fitted on the other half of the pairs (cross-fitting by pair parity), with mean, SD, 95% CI, and the raw SD; then `corrected minus raw mean <d> se <s> t <t>`: the mean over pairs of corrected minus raw points per round, its standard error, and their ratio, the statistic of tests T5 to T7; then the two halves' coefficients (deal, draw, action): first those fitted on the odd pairs and applied to the even pairs, then those fitted on the even pairs and applied to the odd ones. The `# summary` line stays the raw result, and the SPRT runs on the raw pair margin.
- `# time a=<mean>,<max>,<moves> b=<mean>,<max>,<moves>`: each side's reply time per move in milliseconds, from the referee's `go` to the engine's move (mean and slowest), and its number of moves. A record of wall time, never a budget; files written before M4-07 have none.
- `# run run=<id> row=<k> command=<the match command, spaces as +> wall_s=<s> peak_rss_kb=<kb> load=<1-minute load average> date=<YYYY-MM-DD>`: added by a pre-registered run's script (scripts/run-baselines.sh) after the match; peak RSS is what /usr/bin/time -l reports for the match command.

## Equity rounds and tables

crates/dominofish/src/equity.rs (`dominofish equity`; docs/PLAN.md D20). Written under docs/eval/equity/.

- `rounds-<mode>.txt`: the engine's own rounds after the first, from self-play games to 200 between copies of PIMC. The first line is `# equity rounds mode=<mode> target=200 deals=<base>+game engines=<base> worlds=<n> nodes=<cap> table_bits=<b>`: game g deals round r from derive(deals base + g, r), and seat s searches with seeds from derive(derive(engines base, g), 100 + s), reseeded each round as an engine is. Then one line per round, in game order: `round <game> <round> leader <seat> won <0|1> points <p> bonuses <list>`, with the round counted from 1 (always 2 or more), the leader's seat, whether the leader's team won, the round's points (a capicua included), and its pase bonuses in order as L (the leader's team) or O (the other team), or - for none. The round is re-scored from its start with scores 0 and target 300, so every pase shape counts as paid, whatever the cap did in the game. The last line is `# summary rounds=<n> games=<n> moves=<n> decisions=<n> capped=<n>`: the work of the games the rounds came from (decisions: moves searched; a forced move is played without search; capped: solves that reached the node cap). The file holds no wall time or thread count: it is the same at any thread count.
- `w-<mode>-<target>.txt`: W for one target (100 or 200). `# equity table mode=<mode> target=<T> rounds=<n> from=<rounds file>`; `# halves: the tables of rounds 1 to <h> and <h+1> to <n> differ by at most <d> millionths`, the largest difference between the tables built from the two halves of the rounds (their sampling precision, no threshold); then `lead <a> <w_0> ... <w_T-1>` for each own score a from 0 to T - 1, where w_b is the probability in millionths that a team at a, leading the round, wins against a team at b; then the same lines as `other <a> ...` for the team that does not lead.

## Calibration

crates/dominofish/src/calibrate.rs (`dominofish calibrate`; test T8 in docs/PLAN.md). Written under docs/eval/calibration/.

- The first line: `# calibration mode=<mode> target=<T> deals=<base>+game engines=<base> worlds=<n> nodes=<cap> table_bits=<b> table=<hash>`: game g deals round r from derive(deals base + g, r), seat s searches with seeds from derive(derive(engines base, g), 100 + s), and table is the FNV-1a hash of the W table file of the mode and target (docs/eval/equity/w-<mode>-<T>.txt).
- Per game, in game order: `game <g> winner <team> decisions <n>`, then its n searched decisions as `d <g> <round> <ply> team <t> p <p> base <b>`: the round (from 1), the moves already played in it, the mover's team, the probability in millionths that PIMC with the win objective gives that team to win (the mean over the worlds of the move it plays), and the score-only baseline in millionths (W at the start of the round, with both teams' scores then and whether the mover's team led it).
- `# work games=<n> moves=<n> decisions=<n> capped=<n>`, then the summary that `dominofish calibrate --from <file>` recomputes from the lines above: `# summary games=<n> predictions=<n>`; `# brier <B> [lo, hi] base <B0> [lo, hi] skill <S> [lo, hi]`, with 95% intervals from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of whole games (seed 1); and ten lines `# bin <lo>-<hi> count <n> mean <mean prediction> won <share won>` (- for an empty bin). A run script appends a `# run` line, as for match results.

## State text

core/src/position.rs. One line that holds a full state, every hand and the boneyard order included: the bench positions (fixtures/bench.txt, one per line, # for comments) and, from M3-04, the omniscient bot's view. Nothing that sees only an InfoSet reads it.

```text
v=1 mode=<1v1|2v2> target=<t> scores=<s0>,<s1> hands=<n>/<e>/<s>/<w> board=<hex> ends=<LR|--> turn=<seat> starter=<seat> last=<seat|-> passes=<k> phase=<P|R|F> winner=<seat|-> pile=<tiles|->
```

Each hand is a comma-separated tile list (`-` when empty); 1v1 has two hands. board is the board mask in hex (bit i is tile index i). pile is the boneyard in order, the last tile drawn first. A parser rejects any field out of range and any set of tiles that is not a partition of the 28 tiles into the hands, the board, and the boneyard.

## Board requests

crates/dominofish-wasm/src/board.rs, the interface of the web board (web/). The page keeps the game record (Game records, above) and sends one request per call: a command line, a newline, and the record text. The reply is `ok`, a newline, and a body, or `error <reason>`. Commands: `new <1v1|2v2> <100|200> <seed>` (a new record; no record follows), `view` (JSON of the position after the last move: scores, phase, turn, every hand, the line of play with each tile oriented as it lies, the open ends, the boneyard count, the legal moves, the round's history with events, and the round's result), `play <move>` (the record with the move appended; the move must be legal), `undo` (the record without the current round's last move), `next <seed>` (the record with the next round dealt), `bot <name> <seed>` (the move a baseline bot, or DominoFish for `pimc`, makes from the InfoSet of the seat on turn), `engine <seed>` (DominoFish's PIMC analysis from the InfoSet of the seat on turn at 32 worlds: the seat, the worlds, the count of consistent worlds, the solver nodes, the solves that hit the node cap, and each legal move with its mean points for the seat's team and their standard error, best first), and `analyze` (perfect-information analysis: the exact rest-of-round margin, team 0 minus team 1, after each legal move, the best line, and whether a solve hit its node cap).
